Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

100% digital, delivered instantly. We do not sell physical products. Nothing is shipped, so there are no shipping costs, customs fees or delivery times.

How delivery works

Right after your payment is confirmed, you receive an email with a link to your private online delivery page. From there you can view and download every file included in your purchase. Delivery usually takes a few minutes.

What you receive

Each product page lists exactly what is included (number of patterns, guides and bonuses). Files are provided as PDF documents and other standard digital formats that open on computers, tablets and phones. Some patterns are designed to be printed at real scale.

Access to your files

Your delivery page stays available after purchase, so you can come back and download your files again. We recommend saving a copy on your device.

If you don't receive your access

Check your spam or promotions folder first. If the email is not there within 24 hours, email us at support@prorecursos.shop with the email address you used at checkout and we will resend your access link.

Shipping and returns

Because there is no physical shipment, there is nothing to return. If you are not satisfied, our Refund & Cancellation Policy applies (7-day money-back guarantee).

Contact

For any question about delivery, email us at support@prorecursos.shop or use our contact form.
