Refund Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

On this page How to request a refund Processing After 7 days Contact

7-day money-back guarantee. We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you are not satisfied with a product, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase date.

How to request a refund

Send us a message through our contact form with the email address you used to buy and, if you have it, your order number. You do not need to give a reason.

Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Depending on your bank or card issuer, the funds may take 5 to 10 business days to appear in your account.

After 7 days

Because our products are digital and delivered instantly, refund requests received more than 7 days after purchase are not eligible, except where required by applicable law.

Contact

For any question about refunds, send us a message through our contact form.
