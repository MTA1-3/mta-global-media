Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

On this page How to request a refund Processing Cancellations Optional add-ons Returns After 7 days Contact

7-day money-back guarantee. We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you are not satisfied with a product, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase date.

How to request a refund

Email us at support@prorecursos.shop or use our contact form with the email address you used to buy and, if you have it, your order number. You do not need to give a reason.

Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Depending on your bank or card issuer, the funds may take 5 to 10 business days to appear in your account.

Cancellations

All our products, including optional add-ons, are one-time purchases. We do not sell subscriptions and never charge recurring fees, so there is nothing to cancel after you buy. If you want to undo a purchase, request a refund within 7 days as described above.

Optional add-ons

Add-ons accepted after checkout are covered by the same 7-day guarantee and can be refunded on their own, without affecting your main purchase.

Returns

Because our products are digital, there is nothing to ship back. A refund ends your license to use the product.

After 7 days

Because our products are digital and delivered instantly, refund requests received more than 7 days after purchase are not eligible, except where required by applicable law.

Contact

For any question about refunds, email us at support@prorecursos.shop or use our contact form.
