FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I access my purchase?

Right after your payment is confirmed you receive an email with the link to your private delivery page, where you can view and download everything you bought.

I didn't receive my access email. What should I do?

First check your spam or promotions folder. If it isn't there, email us at support@prorecursos.shop or use the contact form with the email you used at checkout, and we will send it again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. You can request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase by emailing support@prorecursos.shop. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.

Are your products physical?

No. All our products are 100% digital. Nothing is shipped, so there are no shipping costs or delivery times.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every product is a one-time payment with no recurring charges, so there is nothing to cancel. Optional add-ons offered after checkout are also one-time payments and are only charged if you accept them.

In which currency will I be charged?

Each store shows its price with the currency stated explicitly (for example, USD 23.99). That is the amount you are charged. Your bank may apply its own conversion fees if your card uses another currency.

Can I share or resell the products?

Products are licensed for personal use only. Sharing, reselling or redistributing them is not allowed. See our Terms of Service.
