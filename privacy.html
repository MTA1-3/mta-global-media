This Privacy Policy explains how MTA Global Media LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our websites or buy our digital products.

1. Information we collect

  • Order information: name, email address, country and the products you purchase.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by third-party payment processors. We do not receive or store your full card details.
  • Usage information: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Communications: messages you send us through our contact form.

2. How we use your information

  • To process your order and deliver your digital products.
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To measure and improve our websites and advertising.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar tools, including advertising pixels from platforms such as Meta, to understand how visitors use our websites and to measure the results of our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate our business (e-commerce platform, payment processors, email and hosting providers, advertising platforms), and only as needed to provide their services, or when required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep order information for as long as necessary to provide our products and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, these rights are provided under the GDPR, and you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any right, email us at support@prorecursos.shop or use our contact form.

7. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our service providers may process data in other countries. When we transfer data internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

8. Children

Our products are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

10. Contact

MTA Global Media LLC, 1057 NW 136th Ave, Miami, FL 33182, United States. For any question about this policy, email us at support@prorecursos.shop or use our contact form.
